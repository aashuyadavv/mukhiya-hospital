Dr. Deeksha Yadav, BAMS, is an Ayurveda physician dedicated to providing thoughtful, patient-centered healthcare. With a focus on holistic wellbeing, she combines Ayurvedic principles with a practical and compassionate approach to individual patient needs. Her aim is to understand each patient’s concerns, provide appropriate guidance, and support long-term health and wellbeing.

About Mukhiya Hospital

A familiar place
to feel looked after.

Mukhiya Hospital is a patient-focused healthcare facility committed to providing compassionate, safe, and professional medical care in a comfortable environment. Our aim is to make every patient feel respected, heard, and well cared for throughout their healthcare journey. With a focus on quality patient care, clear communication, cleanliness, and clinical professionalism, Mukhiya Hospital strives to serve individuals and families with dedication and responsibility.
